## 2. Information handled by the Game

### Local game data

The Game does not require an account or registration. It stores gameplay progress, saved runs, in-game currency, unlocks, achievements, daily-task progress, and preferences such as language and audio settings on your device. The Game does not upload these save files to a game server operated by us and does not currently offer its own cloud-save service.

### Advertising data

The Game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to provide advertising, including optional rewarded ads. Google may automatically collect and share the following information through the advertising SDK, depending on the SDK version, device, settings, and applicable privacy choices:

- **IP address and approximate location:** the IP address can be used to estimate your general location.
- **Device and advertising identifiers:** for example, the Android advertising ID and app set ID, where available and permitted.
- **App and advertising interactions:** such as app launches, ad impressions, taps, and video views.
- **Diagnostic and performance information:** such as launch performance, hangs, energy usage, and information about SDK operation.

These data support ad delivery, ad measurement and analytics, SDK operation, and fraud and abuse prevention. Where permitted and enabled, information may also be used to personalize ads. Rewarded-ad completion is used by the Game to grant the relevant in-game reward.

Advertising SDK processing is separate from your local save files. Declining to watch a rewarded ad does not, by itself, prevent all SDK data processing or delete data already collected.

### Information you send to us

If you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to send. We use this information to respond, resolve the issue, and handle your request.

### Information we do not request

The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location. Its current Google Play implementation does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. This does not mean the advertising SDK collects no diagnostic or interaction data.

## 5. Retention and deletion

- **Local saves and preferences:** these remain on your device until removed or overwritten. You can remove the Game's local data through Android's app-storage settings. Uninstalling normally removes app-local data; device or operating-system backups, if any, are controlled separately and may restore data on reinstall. Clearing local data can permanently erase progress.
- **Support correspondence:** we keep messages only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain necessary support records, and meet applicable legal obligations. We review whether continued retention is necessary. You may request deletion using the email above.
- **Google advertising data:** Google determines retention for data it processes under its policies and applicable service terms. Uninstalling or clearing the Game's data does not automatically erase information already sent to Google. See [Google's data retention explanation](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention) and privacy controls for further information.

There is no Game account to delete. We can handle deletion requests for information under our control, but cannot remotely erase your device's local save files or promise deletion from Google's systems. We will explain the scope of the action we can take and direct you to the relevant provider where appropriate.
